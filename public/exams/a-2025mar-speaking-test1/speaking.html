PART 1Introduction and Interview(考官提问,每题 2–4 句作答)
  1. Let's talk about what you do. Do you work or are you a student?
  2. What's your job? Why did you choose that kind of work?
  3. How long have you been doing it?
  4. Are there things you don't like about it? What are they?
  5. What subjects are you studying?
  6. Why did you choose those subjects? How long have you been studying them?
  7. What do you usually do at weekends?
  8. Do you like weekends?
  9. Do you think it is important to make the most of your weekend?
  10. Which is your favourite part of the weekend? [Why?]
  11. Do you prefer buying things in big companies or from small businesses?
  12. Do you know many small businesses?
  13. Why do some people decide to set up their own business?
  14. What are some of the dangers involved in starting a business?

PART 3Two-way Discussion(双向讨论,围绕 Part 2 话题展开)
  1. Tell me, what do you think are the greatest environmental problems facing your country at present?
  2. And what has been done so far to solve these problems?
  3. How successful would you say these measures have been?
  4. Do you think things are likely to get better or worse in the future?
  5. Is what you are going to study likely to be of any use to solve these problems?
  6. Should countries try to solve climate change problems together or individually? Why?
